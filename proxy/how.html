What happens when someone calls

One caller sentence, start to finish. The whole round trip for the decision takes about a third of a second.

1 · Caller
Says what they need
“my face is swelling”
2 · Avatar's LLM
Hears it and calls one tool: route_request
sends the caller's words
3 · Jev
Answers 4 questions at once. Writes nothing.
emergency: 96%
intent: book
~100 ms
4 · My code
Applies the rules in order
emergency first,
then human,
then intent
5 · The avatar
Jumps to that part of the conversation and talks
next_node:
Emergency Triage
The only thing my server sends back to the avatar is {"next_node": "Emergency Triage"}. Akapulu reads it and moves the conversation there.

Jev is good at

Picking one option, yes or no with a percentage, and scores on a scale. Fast and cheap enough to run on every sentence of a live call.

Jev can't

Write, summarize, do math or compare dates. It can also pick a wrong answer that looks valid. That's why the rules live in code and the talking is done by an LLM.
