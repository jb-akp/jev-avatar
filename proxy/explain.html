When to use it (and when not to)

Jev makes the decision, and a normal model like GPT or Claude does the talking.

Use Jev for

  • Routing calls and tickets
  • Yes or no checks, like "is this urgent?"
  • Scores, like "how angry is this person?"
  • Anything you decide thousands of times

Don't use Jev for

  • Writing or summarizing
  • Math or comparing dates
  • Long chains of reasoning
  • Trusting blindly: it can pick a wrong answer that looks right
